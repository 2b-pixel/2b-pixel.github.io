## Are you defined by your genome?



The question of whether we are defined by our genome touches the core of modern genetics, neuroscience, and philosophy.  I say the genome is just a blueprint us humans tend to stray away from.
While our genome provides the foundational biological framework, who we are as individuals is an emergent outcome of complex interactions between our genes, environment, epigenetics, and choices.


* Most complex human traits (e.g. personality, cognitive abilities, height, and susceptibility to behavioral patterns) re not dictated by a single gene. Instead, they are polygenic, influenced by thousands of genetic variants working in tandem with environmental inputs.  
* Your genotype is also your genetic sequence, with your phenotype is the actual observable physical, psychological, and behavioral outcome. The transition from genotype to phenotype is shaped more so by life experiences.

* If we consider DNA as the hardware, epigenetics will then be the software that determines which genes are turned on, turned off, or dimmed throughout your life. Environmental influences (e.g.stress, diet, trauma, exercise, and sleep) leave chemical marks (like DNA methylation) that alter gene expression without changing the underlying DNA code.
  * Beyond biology lies the human experience: conscious self-awareness, social relationships, personal values, and moral choices.
